On July 19, 2026, Professor Kim Sneppen, Member of the Royal Danish Academy of Sciences and Letters, visited the laboratory for academic exchange. Professor Sneppen discussed ongoing research projects with Yawen Deng, Sen Yang, Xuejiao Wang, Dan Li and Chunmei Zhou, and shared mathematical model of islet synchronization. In the evening, the group headed to the China World Hotel to enjoy the city skyline.
