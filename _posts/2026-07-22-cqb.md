July 22 – The Quantitative Biology 2026: Systems and Physical Biology Conference took place in Beijing from July 20 to 23, 2026. Homeostasis serves as a fundamental prerequisite for sustaining life and human health. Centered on the theme of “Systems and Physical Biology”, the conference aims to explore cutting-edge frontiers in quantitative biology and foster interdisciplinary exchanges among physicists, biologists, mathematicians and computer scientists. Its agenda covers state-of-the-art research across a broad spectrum of fields, including but not limited to: 1. Non-equilibrium dynamics and active biological matter 2. Multiscale biological networks 3. Design principles of living systems 4. Artificial intelligence and biological complexity. Dr. Huixia Ren presented a lecture entitled Global Synchronization of Pancreatic Islets Maintains Fasting Glycemia In Vivo.
